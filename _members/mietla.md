I develop software for autonomous robots, with a focus on drones and ground vehicles. My work covers GNSS-denied navigation with visual-inertial odometry, real-time object detection and tracking on embedded platforms (NVIDIA Jetson, Radxa), and multi-sensor data fusion. I have hands-on experience in building, deploying and evaluating autonomous platforms, from hardware integration to ROS 2 software running on real robots.
