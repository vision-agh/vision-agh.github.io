FP3-IAM4RAIL is one of the flagship projects of the [Europe's Rail Joint Undertaking](https://rail-research.europa.eu/), funded under Horizon Europe. The project develops a holistic and integrated asset management approach for the European railway system, combining advanced asset monitoring, artificial intelligence and digital twins, and linking asset data with traffic management systems. Its results are validated in integrated demonstrators across railway assets.

AGH University of Krakow is one of the project partners.
