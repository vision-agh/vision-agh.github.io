Mateusz Wąsala

MSc Mateusz Wąsala
Research and teaching assistant
PhD Student
wasala@agh.edu.pl

I am interested in embedded vision systems, in particular SLAM algorithms for ambient map generation and positioning of unmanned aerial vehicles. I also design and build equipment for these vehicles.

Computer Vision Event Cameras Hardware Acceleration Perception System Real-Time Processing SLAM SoC FPGA UAVs Visual Odometry

Experience

  1. Research and teaching assistant

    AGH University of Krakow 2021 - Present
    I am conducting research on leveraging SoC computing platforms for autonomous visual perception in unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), with a particular focus on visual odometry and SLAM. My work involves classical algorithms, artificial intelligence methods, hybrid approaches, and event-based cameras. Additionally, I am responsible for conducting laboratory sessions with students on advanced vision systems and perception systems for mobile robotics.

  1. Software Engineer

    BlueCloud Sp. z o.o. June 2014 - October 2019
    I worked as a Software Engineer developing software applications, websites, and scripts using PHP, HTML, JavaScript, and CSS.
