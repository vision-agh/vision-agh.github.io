Konrad Lis

Konrad Lis, MSc
PhD Student
kolis@agh.edu.pl

My research focuses on embedded vision systems and autonomous vehicle perception systems, with particular interest in LiDAR-based solutions. My work covers algorithm development for real-time processing, as well as the implementation and optimization of perception pipelines on embedded platforms.
