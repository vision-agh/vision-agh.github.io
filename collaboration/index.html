Neuro-inspired Perception and Cognition Lab

Czech Technical University in Prague, Czech Republic

Contact person: Giulia D'Angelo

Collaborators: Giulia D'Angelo, Karla Štěpánová, Rayane Rocha, Paolo Ritirato

We explore efficient perception using bio-inspired sensors and neuromorphic computing paradigms, focusing on multimodal event-based visual attention and efficient hardware processing of asynchronous, sparse data.

Neuromorphic Computing Event Camera Visual Attention Spiking Neural Networks

Computer Engineering Group

Paderborn University, Germany

Contact person: Marco Platzner

Collaborators: Christoph Berganski, Felix Jentzsch

We collaborate on compiler optimizations for FPGA dataflow accelerators of quantized neural networks built with the open-source FINN framework. Together we developed scaled-integer range analysis (SIRA), which tracks the range, scale and bias of every tensor to generate smaller and more efficient streaming hardware.

FPGA Quantized Neural Networks Range Analysis Hardware-Aware Compilation

AMD Research & Advanced Development

Contact person: Michaela Blott

Collaborators: Michaela Blott, Yaman Umuroglu, Thomas Preusser, Jakoba Petri-Koenig, Ian Colbert

We collaborate on the efficient deployment of quantized neural networks on FPGAs using the open-source FINN dataflow compiler. Our joint work on scaled-integer range analysis (SIRA) reduces accumulator bitwidths and folds scales, biases and elementwise operations into thresholds, cutting the resource usage of FPGA dataflow accelerators.

FPGA Quantized Neural Networks Dataflow Accelerators FINN Compiler

Computer Architecture Laboratory (CAL)

Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU), Norway

Contact person: Magnus Själander

Collaborators: Magnus Själander, Charalampos Bezaitis, Yaman Umuroglu

We collaborate on energy-efficient hardware for AI at the edge, focusing on FPGA dataflow accelerators for quantized neural networks. Our joint work on scaled-integer range analysis (SIRA) uses interval arithmetic to derive the range, scale and bias of tensors, enabling narrower accumulators and cheaper streaming hardware.

FPGA Edge AI Quantized Neural Networks Energy-Efficient Computer Architecture

Digital Systems and Integrated Circuits Division

Collaborators: Thomas Dalgaty, Thomas Mesquida, Manon Dampfhoffer

Universidad de Sevilla, Spain

Contact person: Antonio Rios-Navarro

Collaborators: Angel Jiménez-Fernández, Antonio Rios-Navarro, Tomás Muñoz, Alejandro Linares-Barranco

We investigate the design of hardware-aware algorithms for event-based data processing on FPGAs, utilising audio data from Neuromorphic Auditory Sensors and visual data from Dynamic Vision Sensors. We combine our expertise in reconfigurable logic design with event-based perception.
