Embedded Perception & Autonomous Systems

Computer Vision Laboratory · Department of Automatic Control and Robotics · Faculty of Electrical Engineering, Automatics, Computer Science and Biomedical Engineering · AGH University of Krakow, Poland

Our research focuses on embedded perception systems that process, in real time and at low power, high-resolution video streams, LiDAR point clouds and data from event cameras – for autonomous mobile robots, including UAVs, UGVs and legged robots.

What we work on

Computer vision

4K video processing, object detection and tracking

Real-time pipelines for high-resolution video, combining classical vision algorithms with deep networks.

Event-based vision

Low-latency perception with event cameras

Perception with event cameras (DVS): filtering, visual odometry, tracking and detection from sparse, microsecond-latency event streams.

Neuromorphic computing

SNNs, event-based audio, neuromorphic control

Spiking Neural Networks, event-based audio processing and neuromorphic control for advanced mobile robotics.

Embedded AI

CNNs and GNNs for real-time, low-power inference

Deep neural networks, including Graph Neural Networks for sparse data, compressed and optimised for on-device deployment.

Hardware acceleration

FPGA, SoC FPGA and embedded GPUs

Zynq, Zynq UltraScale+, Versal, NVIDIA Jetson and emerging neuromorphic hardware.

Autonomous drones

perception and control (classical, MPC, RL)

Perception: detection, tracking and visual navigation. Control: classical, MPC, RL and hybrid controllers.
